## Full description
Need a transcript or captions that are organized and easy to use? I can help with clear recordings, interviews, meetings, voice notes, presentations, and short videos.

**Available deliverables**
- Clean or verbatim transcript
- English or Spanish transcription
- Speaker labels when distinguishable
- Timestamps by agreement
- SRT or WebVTT caption files
- Proofreading and transcript formatting
- Voiceover-ready script formatting
- Optional summary or key points as a separate deliverable

**My process**
1. Confirm language, recording length, speakers, transcript style, and output format.
2. Review the provided recording and prepare a transcript.
3. Check names, numbers, terminology, speaker labels, and uncertain phrases against the media.
4. Validate caption file structure when SRT/VTT is included.
5. Deliver the agreed files and flag any unclear audio.

I will not invent words that cannot be heard. Accuracy depends on recording quality, accents, overlapping speakers, and specialist terminology; difficult audio may require a custom quote.

I do not provide certified legal/medical transcription or promise perfect accuracy. Please share only recordings you have permission to provide.

## FAQ

**Do you work in Spanish and English?**  
Yes. Please specify the language of the recording and whether you need transcription only or translation as a separate service.

**Can you add subtitles?**  
Yes. I can prepare SRT or WebVTT files and check their structure. Final synchronization must be reviewed against the source video.

**What if part of the recording is unclear?**  
I'll flag uncertain words or sections rather than inventing content. Very poor audio may need a custom quote or may be unsuitable.

**Can you transcribe multiple speakers?**  
Yes, when speakers can be distinguished. Please mention overlapping speech or a large number of speakers before ordering.

**Do you guarantee 100% accuracy?**  
No. Audio quality and speech clarity affect accuracy. I review the work carefully and flag uncertainty.
