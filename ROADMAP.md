## Phase 3 — Service quality
- [ ] Add a repeatable checklist for handling uncertain speech and overlapping speakers.
- [ ] Test the validator against a broader set of valid and invalid SRT/WebVTT examples.
- [ ] Document supported caption line-length and reading-speed preferences by client/platform brief.

## Scope guardrails
- Never invent unclear words or claim a fictional or known-script sample was transcribed from real media.
- Never publish a client recording or transcript without permission.
- A validator checks structure, not speech accuracy or synchronization.
- Do not promise perfect accuracy or certified legal/medical transcription.
