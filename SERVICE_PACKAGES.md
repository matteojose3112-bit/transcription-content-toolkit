# Transcription & Content Services — Starter Packages

These are proposed launch packages for Fiverr/Upwork. Confirm the final platform pricing and turnaround before publishing.

## 1. Clean Transcript
**Starter scope:** Up to 10 minutes of clear, single-speaker audio in English or Spanish.
- Clean verbatim transcript in DOCX or TXT
- Basic paragraph and speaker formatting
- One revision for formatting or minor corrections
- Delivery target: 2 business days after receiving a usable recording and confirmed brief

**Not included:** translation, legal/medical certification, heavily noisy audio, multi-hour recordings, or guaranteed identification of unclear speech.

## 2. Transcript + Captions
**Starter scope:** Up to 5 minutes of clear audio/video.
- Clean transcript
- SRT or WebVTT captions
- Basic caption structure and timing checks
- One revision for formatting/timing issues
- Delivery target: 2 business days

**Important:** Caption timing must be reviewed against the actual media. The included validator checks file structure and timing conflicts; it does not determine whether captions are synchronized perfectly or words are accurate.

## 3. Content Repurpose
**Starter scope:** Up to 10 minutes of source material.
- Clean transcript
- Short summary or key points
- Voiceover-ready script formatting, if requested
- Delivery target: 3 business days

## Intake requirements
Ask for the source file or accessible link, language/accent, desired transcript style (verbatim or clean), speaker count if known, timestamps (yes/no), caption format (SRT/VTT), terminology/name spellings, output format, deadline, and confirmation that the client has rights to share the recording.

## Quality and boundaries
- Do not invent unclear words; mark them for review.
- Verify names, numbers, technical terms, speaker labels, and timing against the source.
- Do not promise perfect accuracy or certify legal/medical transcripts.
- Keep client recordings confidential and do not reuse them as portfolio samples without written permission.
- Use a clear revision limit and quote unusually difficult audio separately.
